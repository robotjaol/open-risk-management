# References for Expected Credit Loss under IFRS 9 (Simplified)

## IFRS 9 Financial Instruments, Section 5.5 Impairment

- Authors: IASB, IFRS Foundation
- Year: 2014
- Venue or issuer: Effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2018
- Link: https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2026/issued/ifrs9-ie.html
- Type: regulatory
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: IFRS Foundation illustrative examples page and IASB staff paper, web search 2026-11-25. The link opens the illustrative examples that accompany the standard. Paragraph numbers were not checked.
- Contribution: Sets the expected credit loss model: 12 month ECL when credit risk has not increased significantly, lifetime ECL after a significant increase, and lifetime ECL for credit impaired assets.

## Post-implementation Review of IFRS 9 Impairment: summary of feedback and the IASB's responses

- Authors: IFRS Foundation Trustees, Due Process Oversight Committee staff paper
- Year: 2024
- Venue or issuer: Agenda reference 1C, June 2024
- Link: https://cdn.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/meetings/2024/june/dpoc/ap1c-pir-impairment.pdf
- Type: regulatory
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: IFRS Foundation document, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: Recent official review of how the IFRS 9 impairment model works in practice. Useful for judgement areas such as staging.

## Basel III: Finalising post-crisis reforms (BCBS d424)

- Authors: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision
- Year: 2017
- Venue or issuer: Bank for International Settlements, 7 December 2017, consolidated into the Basel Framework
- Link: https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d424.htm
- Type: regulatory
- Status: integrated into the consolidated Basel Framework
- Verification: verified: BIS page, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: Finalises the Basel III reforms, including the revised standardised approach for credit risk.

## SR 26-2: Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management

- Authors: Federal Reserve, OCC and FDIC
- Year: 2026
- Venue or issuer: 17 April 2026, supersedes SR 11-7 and SR 21-8
- Link: https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2602.htm
- Type: regulatory
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: Federal Reserve page, fetched 2026-11-25
- Contribution: Current US model risk guidance with a risk based approach. The letter says it is most relevant to organisations above 30 billion dollars in assets and states no effective date.
