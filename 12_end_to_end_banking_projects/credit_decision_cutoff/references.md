- Authors: Lessmann, S., Baesens, B., Seow, H.-V. and Thomas, L. C.
- Year: 2015
- Venue or issuer: European Journal of Operational Research 247(1):124-136
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2015.05.030
- Type: academic
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: RePEc IDEAS, dblp and library records, web search 2026-11-25

## Deep learning for credit scoring: Do or don't?

- Authors: Gunnarsson, B. R., vanden Broucke, S., Baesens, B., Óskarsdóttir, M. and Lemahieu, W.
- Year: 2021
- Venue or issuer: European Journal of Operational Research 295(1):292-305
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2021.03.006
- Type: academic
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: RePEc IDEAS, dblp and library records, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: Compares deep learning with conventional and ensemble credit scoring methods. The authors conclude deep learning looks inappropriate there and that XGBoost is preferred when classification performance is the main goal.
