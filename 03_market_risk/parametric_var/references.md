## Generalized autoregressive conditional heteroskedasticity

- Authors: Bollerslev, T.
- Year: 1986
- Venue or issuer: Journal of Econometrics 31(3):307-327
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-4076(86)90063-1
- Type: academic
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: Duke Scholars, RePEc IDEAS, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: Defines the GARCH model for time varying variance. This repository uses it to generate synthetic returns with volatility clustering.

## Techniques for verifying the accuracy of risk measurement models

- Authors: Kupiec, P. H.
- Year: 1995
- Venue or issuer: Finance and Economics Discussion Series 95-24, Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System
- Link: https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/1491/item/717921
- Type: academic
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: Fed FRASER and EconPapers records, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: Introduces the proportion of failures likelihood ratio test for VaR exceptions and discusses its low power on short samples. The Journal of Derivatives version was not checked.

## Coherent measures of risk

- Authors: Artzner, P., Delbaen, F., Eber, J.-M. and Heath, D.
- Year: 1999
- Venue or issuer: Mathematical Finance 9(3):203-228
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9965.00068
- Type: academic
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: RePEc IDEAS and EconPapers records, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: States the axioms of a coherent risk measure and discusses why quantile based measures such as VaR can fail subadditivity.
