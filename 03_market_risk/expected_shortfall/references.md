# References for Expected Shortfall

## On the coherence of expected shortfall

- Authors: Acerbi, C. and Tasche, D.
- Year: 2002
- Venue or issuer: Journal of Banking & Finance 26(7):1487-1503
- Link: https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jbfina:v:26:y:2002:i:7:p:1487-1503
- Type: academic
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: EconPapers and arXiv records, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: Compares definitions of expected shortfall and shows which one is coherent for any loss distribution, including discrete ones.

## Optimization of conditional value-at-risk

- Authors: Rockafellar, R. T. and Uryasev, S.
- Year: 2000
- Venue or issuer: Journal of Risk 2, pp. 21-42 (indexes disagree on the last page)
- Link: https://www.risk.net/journal-risk/2161159/optimization-conditional-value-risk
- Type: academic
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: Risk.net, EconPapers and author PDF, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: Introduces CVaR minimisation through linear programming. CVaR is the same quantity as ES for continuous losses.

## Coherent measures of risk

- Authors: Artzner, P., Delbaen, F., Eber, J.-M. and Heath, D.
- Year: 1999
- Venue or issuer: Mathematical Finance 9(3):203-228
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9965.00068
- Type: academic
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: RePEc IDEAS and EconPapers records, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: States the axioms of a coherent risk measure and discusses why quantile based measures such as VaR can fail subadditivity.

## Elicitability and backtesting: Perspectives for banking regulation

- Authors: Nolde, N. and Ziegel, J. F.
- Year: 2017
- Venue or issuer: The Annals of Applied Statistics 11(4):1833-1874
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1214/17-AOAS1041
- Type: academic
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: Project Euclid, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: Compares traditional backtests with comparative backtests for VaR and ES, and discusses elicitability.

## Backtesting expected shortfall

- Authors: Acerbi, C. and Szekely, B.
- Year: 2014
- Venue or issuer: Risk Magazine, December 2014, pp. 76-81
- Link: no link, verify before citing
- Type: academic
- Status: current
- Verification: verified: Existence confirmed through the reference list of Nolde and Ziegel (2017). The article itself was not opened.
- Contribution: Proposes ES backtest statistics. The Z1 diagnostic in this repository follows the idea in simplified form and is not their full test.

## Minimum capital requirements for market risk (BCBS d457)

- Authors: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision
- Year: 2019
- Venue or issuer: Bank for International Settlements, 14 January 2019 (rev. February 2019), consolidated into the Basel Framework
- Link: https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d457.htm
- Type: regulatory
- Status: integrated into the consolidated Basel Framework
- Verification: verified: BIS page, web search 2026-11-25
- Contribution: Revised market risk framework. The internal models approach uses expected shortfall, and the standard supersedes the January 2016 version (d352).
