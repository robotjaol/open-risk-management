## Limits of version 0.1.0

1. Scope. 8 of 110 planned topics are built. Planned topics have no directory.
2. Data. All results use synthetic or illustrative data. No real portfolio, no real market history. The numbers describe generators written in this repository.
3. Self consistency. Several checks compare a method with data generated by a model of the same family. Example: a Student t VaR fits GARCH data with t shocks well by construction. Agreement shows the code is correct. It does not show the model is right for real markets.
4. Validation. One author built and checked the work. There is no independent validation. The status Complete in this repository means the criteria in [methodology_standards.md](methodology_standards.md) pass. It does not mean the work was independently reviewed.
5. References. Bibliographic records of 16 sources were checked against independent indexes or publisher pages. Three books were not checked and any module citing them stays Validating. Checking a record is not the same as reading the full text for every claim.
6. Regulation. Texts change. SR 11-7 was superseded by SR 26-2 on 17 April 2026 according to the Federal Reserve page used. Zone limits in the Basel traffic light function and IFRS 9 details must be rechecked against the current primary text.
7. Statistical power. Backtests with 250 days have low power. The backtesting module measures this by simulation.
8. Known failed check. `credit_scoring` reports `auc_within_0.01_of_raw_logistic` as not met: the scorecard test AUC is 0.7603 against 0.7805 for the raw logistic model on the same split. The threshold of 0.01 was set before the run and was not changed. Likely cause (inference, not isolated in this version): six quantile bins per input lose ranking information. The rule of thumb thresholds for PSI and IV come from practitioner sources that were not verified.
9. Simplifications. ECL uses a constant hazard, no prepayment, no cure rate, no collateral timing and no off balance sheet exposure. VaR modules use linear portfolios only. Duration covers parallel shifts only and no embedded options.
10. Estimation floor. The Student t degrees of freedom estimated on the first 1000 days is 2.07 and is floored at 3.0 in the market modules, because the variance is not finite below 2 and the t VaR formula needs it. This choice affects every t result and is disclosed in each module.

## Assumptions

* Fact: the limits above were taken from the saved results files and the code.
* Inference: results would differ on real data. Direction and size are unknown.

## Check before use

* Rerun the notebooks yourself. Compare your results JSON with the committed one.
* Before any real use, replace synthetic data with your own and have the model validated under your institution's policy.
